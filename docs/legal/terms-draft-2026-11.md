## Questions for your review

1. **Indemnity (section 5).** Does the wording meet the Texas fair-notice rules (express negligence and conspicuousness)? Does the choice of Texas law matter for a Virginia LLC with Houston operations?
2. **Non-circumvention (section 6).**
   - A conversion fee for hiring a driver could be read as FleetGrid supplying labor, which cuts against "not a staffing agency" in section 1. Does it weaken that position?
   - Could a limit on hiring drivers raise no-poach or antitrust concerns, or limits under Texas law on restrictive covenants, even though it binds carriers only?
   - We propose that "introduced" means the first time the driver books a shift with that carrier: it is narrow, provable from the booking record, and works whether or not carrier search moves to counts. Is that definition sound? Note the trade-off: a carrier that finds a driver through search and hires them without ever booking a shift is not covered.
3. **Roster-Ready and Documented profile (section 3).** Do these labels create any implied representation or duty of care, given that FleetGrid reviews profiles and keeps documents? Are there negligent-selection risks in the review step?
4. **Consumer reports.** Driver cards show self-reported driving-record and credential answers to subscribing carriers. Does this raise any Fair Credit Reporting Act question?
5. **Worker classification.** Do the booking rules in section 7 (automatic pause for no-shows, offers sent by FleetGrid) sit comfortably with "does not supervise", under federal and Texas tests?
6. **Medical card access (section 4).** Carriers view a medical card for a booked shift, up to 7 days after it ends. Any privacy limits to reflect here or in the Privacy Policy?
7. **Arbitration (section 14).** Keep the current AAA clause and class waiver, or courts? If arbitration stays, does anything need to differ for drivers and for carriers?
8. **Liability cap for drivers (section 12).** The cap is fees paid, which is zero for drivers. Keep the current $100 floor?
9. **Per-fill billing (section 4).** $35 per completed fill ties FleetGrid's fee to a driver working a shift. Does that weaken "technology platform only" or "not a staffing agency" in section 1, or affect broker status? What does the auto-renewal disclosure need to say for a card-on-file trial?
10. **Planned driver consent forms.** Two forms are planned and need your wording: an FCRA authorization for MVRs ordered by carriers, and a Clearinghouse limited-query consent. Who should present and keep them, the carrier or FleetGrid, and does holding them change FleetGrid's position under question 4?
11. **"Cleared for [Carrier]" (section 3).** A planned label, based only on what a carrier tells FleetGrid. Could showing it create a representation by FleetGrid, or a risk if a carrier's statement is wrong?
