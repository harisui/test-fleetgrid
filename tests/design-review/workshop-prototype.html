Premium/Mature: restrained and official

Sharp edges, calm colors, built like equipment. Three palettes share the same screens: Interstate (DOT navy and graphite), Guide Sign (highway guide green) and Workshop (graphite and burnt orange). Each has a light and a dark mode. Shapes, type, motion and copy are shared: 4 to 6px radii, 1 to 2px borders, flat surfaces, 18px body, 48 to 56px targets, no bounce. Cards and chips are live.
